# Response to the Editor — FHOPS (SOFTX-D-26-00697R2)

**Manuscript:** FHOPS: a reproducible optimisation and benchmarking stack for forest harvest operations
**Decision:** Minor revision
**Editor:** Randall Sobie (Editor-in-Chief, SoftwareX)

We thank the editor for these helpful suggestions. All of them have been adopted; the changes
are limited to the abstract, one highlight, and one sentence of the introduction, and are shown
in the marked-up manuscript.

### E.1 "It is difficult to refer to other works in the abstract, so it is better to be more general. (I am also uneasy with you refering to the work of one of the authors in the abstract)"

**Response:** Agreed. The abstract no longer refers to the systematic review (or to any other
work). The review is cited only in the introduction, where the self-citation is disclosed.

### E.2 Suggested combined first sentence

**Response:** Adopted verbatim: "Operational forest machine scheduling remains dominated by
site-specific tools that are difficult to transfer, audit, and compare across studies, giving
rise to calls for reusable, transparent planning workflows."

### E.3 "Our journal is multidisciplinary and the abstract has words and acronyms that are unknown me (as a non-expert) "SA/ILS/Tabu heuristics)" ?"

**Response:** The heuristics are now spelled out ("simulated annealing, iterated local search,
and tabu search heuristics"). We also replaced other specialist wording in the abstract
("scenario contract" is now "scenario data format", "dataset ladder" is now "datasets of
increasing size", and "synthetic scaling tiers" is now "synthetic instances for scaling tests"),
and in the highlights "MILP solvers" is now "exact solvers". The acronyms SA, ILS and Tabu are
now also defined at their first use in the main text (the caption of Table 1 and Section 2).

### E.4 "You could drop "(tiny7, small21, med42)" as well."

**Response:** Dropped, together with the abbreviation "(BC)", which the abstract did not use
again.

### E.5 "Main -20- you could drop the initials in the bracket since you mention that it is from all 3 authors"

**Response:** Dropped. The sentence now reads "Because all three present authors are co-authors
of that review, we disclose the self-citation up front …".
