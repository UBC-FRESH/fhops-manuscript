\textbf{Re: SOFTX-D-26-00697R2, ``FHOPS: a reproducible optimisation and benchmarking stack for forest harvest operations''}

Dear Dr. Sobie,

Please find enclosed the revised manuscript, together with a marked-up manuscript and a point-by-point response to your comment and those of both reviewers.

We thank you and the reviewers for their positive assessment. As you suggested, the operational MILP formulation has been moved out of the main text: the complete labelled formulation is now versioned with the software (FHOPS~1.0.1) and cited, a compact appendix summarises it with an equation-to-code table, and a concise summary is kept in Section~2. In response to Reviewer~\#2 we have:
\begin{itemize}
  \item clarified where the operational model sits in the planning hierarchy and its scope boundary at the landing;
  \item explained how utilisation and delays are represented, how coupled and decoupled systems can be compared, and how rolling-horizon re-solving is supported;
  \item revised the keywords, expanded the FHOPS acronym in the abstract, and redrawn the playback figure for legibility.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Software correction (FHOPS~1.0.1).} We want to draw your attention to one further change. The careful review prompted us to re-examine the code paths behind several reviewer questions and then to audit FHOPS~1.0.0 more broadly. In doing so we found defects:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the rolling-horizon driver did not carry block progress, staged inventories, machine positions, or locks from one planning window to the next;
  \item the stochastic playback applied landing shocks and downtime durations incorrectly, and warm-starting the MILP with the default open-source solver (HiGHS) failed;
  \item several MILP constraint blocks did not match the rules used by the heuristics and playback (staged inventories per upstream role, landing capacity per shift and binding by default, moves across idle shifts, the loader truckload threshold, and loader batching variables that imposed nothing);
  \item the SA, ILS, and Tabu heuristics could report an objective higher than that of the schedule they returned, and their repair step could overload landings or starve downstream roles.
\end{itemize}
Rather than defer or work around these defects, we fixed them, added regression tests, tightened scenario validation, retired an unused legacy MIP model, and published FHOPS~1.0.1, which the revised manuscript now cites. Appendix~A states the corrected formulation.
