\textbf{Re: SOFTX-D-26-00697R2, ``FHOPS: a reproducible optimisation and benchmarking stack for forest harvest operations''}

Dear Dr. Sobie,

Please find enclosed the revised manuscript, together with a marked-up manuscript and a point-by-point response to your comment and those of both reviewers.

We thank you and the reviewers for their positive assessment. As you suggested, the complete operational MILP formulation and its equation-to-code mapping now appear in an appendix, with a concise summary kept in Section~2. In response to Reviewer~\#2 we have:
\begin{itemize}
  \item clarified where the operational model sits in the planning hierarchy and its scope boundary at the landing;
  \item explained how utilisation and delays are represented, how coupled and decoupled systems can be compared, and how rolling-horizon re-solving is supported;
  \item revised the keywords, expanded the FHOPS acronym in the abstract, and redrawn the playback figure for legibility.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Software correction (FHOPS~1.0.1).} We want to draw your attention to one further change. The careful review prompted us to re-examine the code paths behind several reviewer questions, and in doing so we found defects in FHOPS~1.0.0:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the rolling-horizon driver did not carry block progress, staged inventories, or machine positions from one planning window to the next;
  \item the stochastic playback applied landing shocks and downtime durations incorrectly;
  \item warm-starting the MILP with the default open-source solver (HiGHS) failed.
\end{itemize}
Rather than defer or work around these defects, we fixed them, added regression tests, and published FHOPS~1.0.1, which the revised manuscript now cites. The deterministic benchmark, tuning, and scaling results are reproduced exactly under FHOPS~1.0.1 and are unchanged. Because of the playback fix, the stochastic utilisation values in Section~3.2 changed slightly (by at most 0.02); the conclusions drawn from them are unchanged.
