% Appendix A: compact summary of the FHOPS 1.0.1 operational MILP. The complete canonical
% formulation is versioned with FHOPS (cited below); its labels must match this summary.
This appendix summarises the deterministic operational MILP of FHOPS~1.0.1. The complete canonical formulation, with all sets, parameters, decision variables, constraint equations and their exact linearisations, defaults, and the list of changes from FHOPS~1.0.0, is versioned with the software at tag \texttt{v1.0.1}~\cite{fhops2026formulation} and rendered in the FHOPS documentation~\cite{fhops2026formulationdocs}. The labels below are those of that source. Table~\ref{tab:formulation-map} gives the meaning of each labelled block and the Pyomo components that implement it in \breakpath{src/fhops/model/milp} (\texttt{operational.py}, \texttt{data.py}, \texttt{driver.py}).

\paragraph{Notation}
Machines $m\in\mathcal{M}$, blocks $b\in\mathcal{B}$, landings $\ell\in\mathcal{L}$, and shift slots $s=(d,\sigma)\in\mathcal{S}$ (day $d$, shift $\sigma$) in time order. The harvest system of block $b$ defines its required roles $\mathcal{R}_b$, its terminal roles $\mathcal{T}_b\subseteq\mathcal{R}_b$, and the upstream roles of each role. The data are machine production rates $\bar{p}_{mb}$, block volumes $W_b$, availability $A_{m,s}\in\{0,1\}$ (calendars and blackouts), block windows, landing capacities $C_\ell$, truckload volumes $q^{\text{batch}}_b$, head-start buffers $B_{r,b}$, mobilisation costs $\delta_{m,b',b}$, and objective weights $\omega^{\text{prod}},\omega^{\text{mob}},\omega^{\text{trans}},\omega^{\text{land}}$. The decision variables are assignments $x_{m,b,s}\in\{0,1\}$, machine production $p_{m,b,s}\ge 0$, role production $z_{r,b,s}\ge 0$, moves $y_{m,b',b,s}\ge 0$ ($b'\ne b$) of a machine-position network, staged inventories $I_{u,b,s}\ge 0$ per upstream role $u$, activation, waiver, and loader-tail binaries $g$, $h$, $\lambda$, leftover volumes $L_b\ge 0$, and unit landing-surplus slacks $S_{\ell,s,k}\in[0,1]$, $k=1,\dots,K_\ell$.

\paragraph{Objective (OBJ)}
\begin{multline*}
\max\; \omega^{\text{prod}}\!\sum_{b}\sum_{r\in\mathcal{T}_b}\sum_{s} z_{r,b,s}
- \omega^{\text{prod}}\!\sum_{b} L_b
- \omega^{\text{land}}\!\sum_{\ell,s}\sum_{k=1}^{K_\ell} k\,S_{\ell,s,k}\\
- \sum_{m,s}\sum_{b'\ne b}\bigl(\omega^{\text{mob}}\delta_{m,b',b}+\omega^{\text{trans}}\bigr)\,y_{m,b',b,s}.
\end{multline*}
\textbf{OBJ} rewards terminal-role production (machine production on blocks without terminal roles) and subtracts unmet block volume, an increasing price for each machine beyond landing capacity, and the cost of every move between blocks. The optional earliness stage (\textbf{OBJ2}; default in rolling-horizon windows) re-solves the model with \textbf{OBJ} held within $10^{-6}\max(1,|z_1|)$ of its first-stage value $z_1$ and maximises production-weighted earliness, so ties in \textbf{OBJ} are broken towards earlier production.

\paragraph{Constraint blocks}
The labels \textbf{E1}--\textbf{E11} are those of FHOPS~1.0.0. \textbf{E6}, \textbf{E7}, \textbf{E8} (now \textbf{E8a}--\textbf{E8c}), \textbf{E9}, and \textbf{E11} were reformulated in FHOPS~1.0.1, and \textbf{E12}, \textbf{E13}, \textbf{INIT}, and \textbf{OBJ2} are new.
